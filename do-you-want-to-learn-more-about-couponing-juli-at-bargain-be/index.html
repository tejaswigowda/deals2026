Do You Want to Learn More About Couponing? Juli at Bargain Believer is Offering a Free Online Course That's Usually $20 (You'll Have Access for 30 Days)!

March 19, 2020 · Alicia#Deals in Arizona

If you've ever wanted to learn how to be better at grocery store couponing to the extreme, my friend Juli at Bargain Believer is an expert! She wrote me yesterday and let me know that she's offering her online course for free for 30 days to anyone who would like to learn (she usually charges $20). Head over here to get signed up. The course is taught in 23 separate modules. It's over 4 1/2 hours of videos, but is also available in written format for those who prefer to read or run through it it faster.

She offers a Beyond Membership for $7/month that includes really easy coupon matchups (she and her sidekick Gina spend hours and hours on this), but you definitely do not need to sign up in order to take the course.

I'm all about helping each other through this difficult time.

Thanks Juli!
