Check Out the Free Subway Dbacks Fan Fest Monday!

February 20, 2017 · Alicia Markham

Head over here for more information about the Subway Dbacks Fan Fest on Monday (February 20th).

"The Arizona Diamondbacks will host the 13th annual SUBWAY® D-backs Fan Fest (#DbacksFanFest) on Monday, February 20, 2017 from noon to 4 p.m. at Salt River Fields at Talking Stick. Early entry will begin at 11 a.m. for Season Ticket Holders. The free event will offer fans access to current players, coaches, alumni and broadcasters during the SUBWAY® stage show and autograph and photo sessions."
