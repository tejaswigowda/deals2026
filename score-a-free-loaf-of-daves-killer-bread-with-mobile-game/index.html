Score a FREE Loaf of Dave's Killer Bread With Mobile Game!

March 19, 2021 · Alicia

Head over here to check out an easy way to score a free loaf of Dave's Killer Bread (or English muffins or bagels). You turn on your camera and scan the QR code to get to the game link. You'll put together a sandwich and earn a free loaf of bread (you can also play to win a year's worth of bread and/or $5000). If you mess up, don't worry you can play again! They e-mailed me my coupon within around 10 minutes of winning.
