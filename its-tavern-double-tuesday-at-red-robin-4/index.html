It's Tavern Double Tuesday at Red Robin!

September 22, 2015 · Alicia Markham

It's Tavern Double Tuesday at participating Red Robin restaurants (9/22). Stop by to get a free Tavern Double with bottomless steak fries with the purchase of an entree and two beverages. You may want to call your Red Robin ahead of time to make sure they're participating.

Enjoy!
