Mark Your Calendar for the Free Entrance Days to the National Parks in 2020!

January 5, 2020 · Alicia

Mark your calendars for the 2020 free entrance days to the National Parks!

  • January 20: Birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr.
  • April 18: First day of National Park Week
  • August 25: National Park Service Birthday
  • September 26: National Public Lands Day
  • November 11: Veterans Day

Head over here for more information.

If you have a fourth grader this year, don’t forget about the Every Kid in a Park program! Head over here to request a pass for them to get up to three adults and unlimited kids (or a whole car full) into every national park through August 2020.
