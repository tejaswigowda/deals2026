First Friday FREE Admission to the Children's Museum of Phoenix, the Heard Museum, and the Phoenix Art Museum the Night of June 7th!

June 4, 2024 · Alicia#Deals in Arizona#Free#HOT DEAL

Would you like to take your family out for a great night of learning and fun FOR FREE? Check out First Friday in downtown Phoenix! This Friday, June 7th, three fantastic museums will offer everyone free admission from 5-9 PM.

First on the list is the Children's Museum of Phoenix! This fun and interactive place is perfect for the little ones in your life. Admission is usually $17/each (adults must pay too). Click here to read what the Children's Museum of Phoenix wrote about Free Friday capacity limits and recommendations.

Next up is the Phoenix Art Museum. Four times a year they offer free admission on Frist Friday from 5-9 and 6/7 is one of the dates (also mark your calendar for 9/6 and 12/6)! They promise live music, art-making workshops, entertainment, and more. Tickets are usually $28 for adults and $18 for kids, but know that even on First Friday you'll have to pay for the Barbie special exhibit (click here to see the details).

Last but certainly not least on the list is the Heard Museum. This is a beautiful place with an incredible collection of American Indian Art and much more. For First Friday they promise quite a dance party (click here for the details) and I called to confirm that admission is free for all on 6/7 from 5-9 PM. Admission is usually $22.50 for adults and $9 for kids.

Enjoy!
