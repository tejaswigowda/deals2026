Get in FREE to the National Parks Saturday September 24th!

September 23, 2016 · Alicia Markham

Saturday, September 24th is National Public Lands Day and to celebrate you can get into the National Parks including the Grand Canyon and lots of other great AZ parks FOR FREE!

Head over here for more information and keep in mind that you can also get in free November 11th in honor of Veterans Day.

Check out the national parks in AZ:

If you have a child in the fourth grade, head over here to score a free family pass good for admission to the parks for this entire school year!
