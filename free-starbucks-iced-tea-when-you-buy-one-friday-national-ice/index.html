Here's the official info they sent over:

"National Iced Tea Day Deal at Starbucks Retail Stores

Visit Starbucks® Stores to Buy One Teavana® Shaken Iced Tea, Get One Free

Buy one freshly brewed Teavana Shaken Iced Tea at participating Starbucks stores, and get one FREE to share with a friend. Celebrate National Iced Tea Day at Starbucks with a refreshing handcrafted beverage such as the Teavana Shaken Iced Mango Black Tea Lemonade or the Teavana Shaken Iced Peach Green TeaLemonade.

WHEN: Friday, June 10

WHERE: Participating U.S. Starbucks stores

Free iced tea beverage must be of equal or lesser value. Cannot be combined with other offers or discounts."
