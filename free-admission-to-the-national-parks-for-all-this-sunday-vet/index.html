FREE Admission to the National Parks for All This Sunday (Veterans Day)!

November 9, 2018 · Alicia

If you're looking for something fun to do this Veterans Day weekend, why not head to a National Park?! Admission is FREE on Sunday, November 11th for all.

If you know a 4th grader, let them know they can get their family into the national parks free all year with the Every Kid in a Park program, they can head over here to sign up!

If you live in AZ and haven't been to the Grand Canyon, this is a perfect opportunity!

Enjoy!
