Mark Your Calendar! FREE Pizza or Salad at Fired Pie in Phoenix Saturday, August 23rd from 11:30-6!

August 15, 2014 · Alicia Markham

Next weekend I've got a meal covered for you! Head to the newest Fired Pie in Collier Center in Phoenix (201 East Washington Street #111) for a FREE pizza or salad from 11:30-6 on Saturday, August 23rd. The freebie is good at this location only for their grand opening!

They'll cut off the line at 6 so I would get there early to make sure you get your free pizza or salad!

You get to choose your own dough, sauce, and toppings (kind of like Chipotle for pizza).

Enjoy!
