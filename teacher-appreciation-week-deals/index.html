Teacher Appreciation Week Deals!

May 1, 2017 · Alicia Markham

Hooray! This is the week we get to celebrate the wonderful teachers in our lives! I am so thankful to all of the fabulous teachers who have helped Trey and Kaitlyn and all of the other kids in our lives through the years.

Check out some deals that valley businesses are offering teachers this week (this is one post you may want to forward...spread the savings!).

Organ Stop Pizza: Monday May 1st through Friday May 5th, 2017 all qualifying* teachers will receive a voucher for a FREE pizza of choice as our way of saying THANK YOU for all you do!

*All teachers must be a currently employed K-12 level teacher (including Homeschool) with valid ID. Sorry, working at a school in a position other than a teacher does not qualify.

*Free pizzas are for dine-in only.

*Vouchers expire 8/31/17.

*Vouchers will only be distributed during regular posted business hours below.

  • Monday 5/1, 5:00pm – 9:00pm
  • Tuesday 5/2 5:00pm – 9:00pm
  • Wednesday 5/3, 5:00pm – 9:00pm
  • Thursday 5/4, 5:00pm – 9:00pm
  • Friday 5/5, 5:00pm – 10:00pm"

Sauce Pizza and Wine: From Monday, May 1 to Friday, May 5, Sauce is celebrating with 15-percent off for for teachers who dine-in at any of their locations (excluding Sky Harbor)! A teacher just needs to mention the offer and show their school ID to enjoy their perk!

Chipotle: Teachers, faculty, and staff: bring in your school ID to any Chipotle in the U.S. on Tuesday May 2nd from 3pm-close to receive BUY-ONE/GET-ONE FREE BURRITOS, BOWLS, SALADS, OR ORDERS OF TACOS.

Sardella's Pizza and Wings: "Teachers, faculty and school staff who show a valid school ID at any Sardella’s Pizza and Wings location on May 3, 2017 can take advantage of a special promotion in honor of Teacher Appreciation Week and receive a Medium 12” Cheese Pizza for only $4*. This promotion is valid at all Sardella’s Pizza and Wings locations from 4:00 p.m.to close."

Dunkin Donuts: "In honor of National Appreciation Day, Dunkin’ Donuts is saying thank you to local teachers with medium hot or iced coffees for only 25 cents all day long at locations across the Valley on Tuesday, May 9! All teachers need to do to redeem the offer is show their teacher ID card."

If you know of others please e-mail me (alicia@dealsinaz.com) and I'll add them to the list!

Enjoy!
