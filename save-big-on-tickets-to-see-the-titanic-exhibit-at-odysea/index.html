Save Big on Tickets to See the Titanic Exhibit at OdySea!

May 15, 2019 · Alicia

Any Titanic fans out there besides me?! You'll definitely want to check out the Titanic Exhibit going on at OdySea! Head over here for discount tickets, you'll pay $25 for an adult including audio tour (brings it to life), or $20 for a child including audio tour.

These tickets are good any day you choose through 11/1/2019.

Save them for a really hot summer day!

  • "What’s included: admission to Titanic Exhibition with audio tour
  • Adult tickets valid for customers ages 13–64; child tickets valid for customers ages 2–12
  • Free admission for children under 2

Titanic: The Artifact Exhibition takes guests on a journey back in time to experience the legend of Titanic like never before. The galleries in this fascinating Exhibition feature over 137 real artifacts, including 120 never seen before in Arizona, recovered from the ocean floor along with room re-creations and personal stories; each highlighting a different chapter in the compelling story of Titanic’s maiden voyage."

We went to see this in San Diego a few years ago and learned a ton. It definitely brings this tragedy to life (and I guarantee you'll make sure any ship you get on in the future has enough lifeboats for everyone).

Enjoy!
