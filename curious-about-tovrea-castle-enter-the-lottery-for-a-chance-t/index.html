Curious About Tovrea Castle? Enter the Lottery for a Chance to Buy Tickets in the Fall (They've Always Been Sold Out When I've Tried to Go in the Past)!

June 1, 2022 · Alicia

This is definitely a bucket list thing for all of us Phoenicians! Head over here to enter the lottery for the chance to buy tickets in the fall. You can choose 1, 2, 3, or 4 tickets and three dates that work for you. Tickets are $22/each and if you win you'll still have to pay, but the "deal" is that you'll be able to get tickets (they've always been sold out for me). If you win, you'll have 72 hours to pay for your tickets (if your plans have changed before that, you're not out any money). Head over here to learn more about the Castle on the hill you've probably driven by thousands of times!
