Don't Pay $100 For Two General Admission Tickets to Waste Management Phoenix Open...Get Them FREE At Tempe Marketplace or Desert Ridge With Purchase!

January 31, 2018 · Alicia

If you’re thinking about going to the Phoenix Open this week, don’t pay for tickets! At Desert Ridge Marketplace and Tempe Marketplace you can get two free tickets (worth $100) when you spend $300 in one day. You can buy gift cards (even Visa gift cards you can use anywhere Visa is accepted), groceries, gas, anything you can think of there!

I just called Desert Ridge Marketplace (480) 513-7586 and Tempe Markeplace (480) 966-9338 to confirm that they do have tickets available as of right now. You may want to call before you make a special trip.

At Desert Ridge "same day receipts totaling $300 or more can be redeemed at the Desert Ridge Marketplace Management Office located on the north side of The District between Xi Clothing and AT&T, Monday through Friday from 8am-5pm. "

At Tempe Marketplace "receipts can be redeemed at the Management Office, located near Harkins Theatres just north of Yogis Grill, Monday through Friday from 8:30am-5pm."

Enjoy!
