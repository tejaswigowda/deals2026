The Pogo Pass. Just in time for Spring Break it's now good at another venue, Makutu's Island! Each pass holder will get in for free twice during the year Monday-Friday, the place is lots of fun for the family. It has indoor trees full of fun and adventure, a zip line and a lot more. Admission is usually $10. You'll still get into the Phoenix Zoo twice, Castles and Coasters, the IDEA museum, Enchanted Island, Phoenix Rock Gym, Skateland, and lots more. Head over here and with code ALICIA you'll pay just $39.98 per pass, a $99.95 value. This is truly the ultimate spring break staycation-maker! Head over here for more information about the Pogo Pass and everywhere you can use it! You're automatically entered to win a family four pack of tickets to see Wicked at Gammage with each pass purchase!

Want to go swimming? Take the kids to the Kiwanis wave pool in Tempe. It's indoors and the pool is heated. There's a big water slide, I used to go here all the time when I was a kid. Through March 19th you'll get up to two kids 7 and younger in for free with each adults admission. Adults are $4 and kids are usually $3.

Take a Road Trip! You can get a two-night stay in a cabin near the Grand Canyon Sky Walk at Grand Canyon Ranch Resort for as low as $89 (HALF OFF). There is a that includes an evening camp fire with smores and entertainment and spring water in the rooms. The place averages 4 stars on Trip Advisor and they are filling up fast.

Take the kids to the Renaissance Festival on the weekend! Head to Wendy's or Schlotzsky's or select other AZ stores for coupons for buy one get one free tickets! You'll get a free child admission with an adult admission purchase.

Sounds like a GREAT spring break!
