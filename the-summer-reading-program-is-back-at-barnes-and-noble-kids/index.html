The Summer Reading Program is BACK at Barnes and Noble! Kids Going into 1st-6th Grades Can Earn a Free Book!

June 19, 2020 · Alicia

If you have a child going into 1st-6th grade, head over here for more information about the summer reading program going on at Barnes and Noble. Kids going into 1st-6th grades can read 8 books, fill out this form, and bring it into a B&N store August 1st-31st for a free book (there is a list to choose from).

Enjoy!
