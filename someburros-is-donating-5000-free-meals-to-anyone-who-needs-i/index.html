Someburros is Donating 5000 Free Meals to "Anyone Who Needs It" on Wednesday!

May 26, 2020 · Alicia#EXPIRED#Free

The people at Someburros truly have hearts of gold. They recognize that times have been hard lately for everyone and they are offering 5000 hot and ready to eat meals to "anyone who needs it". The event is happening Wednesday, May 27th from 3-7 PM in the Tempe Diablo Stadium parking lot. Each meal box will include a bean and cheese burro, rice, chips, and their famous hot sauce. Each car can have up to six meals with no questions asked. They recommend arriving early as the meals may go quickly.

Someburros loves the community and wants to help make life a little bit better for the people who live here. If you are a family in need, or could just use an emotional boost (I think we all could these days), Someburros hopes to see you on Wednesday.
