Amazon Prime members can head over here to get 3 months of Audible FOR FREE! You'll get to choose a new title each month (includes best sellers and new releases). You'll keep the titles you choose for as long as you have an Audible account (though you can cancel at any time). If you decide you love it, you'll pay $8.95 a month after the three-month trial ends. I just signed up and am currently listening to my first pick The Calamity Club: A Novel by Kathryn Stockett (author of The Help). Without the free trial, I would have paid $27.63 for the Audiobook. The narrators are excellent...it's so fun to listen to in the car (or on my Alexa when I'm folding laundry). If you have a road trip coming up, you could listen to any book you've been meaning to read. I did an Audible trial a few years ago and didn't renew. I listened to Atomic Habits last time, and to this day, I do 100 jumping jacks every morning because of it. A three-month trial is a wonderful pre-Prime Day surprise, and I'm already enjoying it. Hope you enjoy too!
