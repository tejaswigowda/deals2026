If you have Prime, head over here to sign up to earn a $10 credit on Amazon! All you need to do is earn four stamps...you'll get a stamp for making a Prime-eligible purchase, a stamp for streaming a show on Prime Video, a stamp for listening to a song with Prime Music, and a stamp for borrowing a book with Prime Reading.

It's a great way to earn an easy $10 AND to check out the best features of Prime if you're not already using them! Check out how I earned my $10 credit:

I bought 3 triple rolls of Sparkle Paper Towels for $5.28 shipped with Prime (remember Amazon can raise prices at any moment). You can make any eligible Prime purchase over $5 to get the stamp!

I streamed an episode of Upload, a quirky Amazon Original I really enjoyed. You can stream ANY show on Prime Video to get the stamp (they say it can take 48 hours for this stamp to show up).

I streamed an Ed Sheeran song on Prime Music. You can listen to ANY song included with Prime that you like!

I borrowed The Good Lie on Prime Reading. It looks kind of dark but I do love Dateline so we'll see. You can borrow any book available on Prime Reading that looks good to you!

And just like that, I earned a $10 credit!

Keep in mind that Prime Day (s) is coming July 12th & 13th! We can look forward to epic deals (according to them)!

Enjoy!
