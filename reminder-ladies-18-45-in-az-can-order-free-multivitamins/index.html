Reminder: Ladies 18-45 in AZ Can Order FREE Multivitamins!

November 13, 2014 · Alicia Markham

Just a quick reminder that if you're an Arizona woman between the ages of 18 and 45, you can head over here to take a quiz and request FREE multivitamins from the Arizona Department of Health Services. When your vitamins run out, you can order them again! I just got mine in the mail (again)! You'll also get a nail file, a bag, and a healthy living booklet.

Here's to good health!

Enjoy!
