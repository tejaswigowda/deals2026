Happy First Friday! FREE Admission to the Children's Museum of Phoenix, the Phoenix Art Museum, and More!

March 6, 2015 · Alicia Markham

Hooray! Today (3/6) is First Friday! If you don’t have plans, you may want to head to Downtown Phoenix to enjoy the gorgeous weather, some beautiful art, an Ollie the Trolley ride, and some FREE museums! Anyone that says Phoenix has no culture has obviously never been to First Friday!

Head to the Children’s Museum of Phoenix where everyone will get in free from 5-9 PM. Tickets are usually $11 each, so this is quite an opportunity to play for less!

Still have energy? Go to the Phoenix Art Museum for their First Friday event. Everyone will get in for free from 6-10 PM (tickets are usually $15 for adults, $6 for kids). Head over here for more information and to see exhibits on display (Andy Warhol will be available for $2 tonight only).

If it's date night, you might want to think about checking out the Arizona Science Center. Adults 18 and over will get in for free (general admission). Head over here for more information and to check out the lecture series.

Note: The Heard isn't participating this month but will be back in April!

Enjoy and have fun!
