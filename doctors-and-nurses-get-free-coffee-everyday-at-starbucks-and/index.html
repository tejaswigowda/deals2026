Doctors and Nurses Get Free Coffee Everyday at Starbucks, Free Coffee, Tea, and Polar Pops at Circle K Everyday, and a Free Dozen Doughnuts at Krispy Kreme on Mondays!

Starbucks has announced that through May 3rd anyone who identifies as a Covid-19 front line responder will get a free tall hot or iced coffee. Head over here to read the details.
