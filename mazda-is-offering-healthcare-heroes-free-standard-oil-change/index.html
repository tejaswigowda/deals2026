Mazda is Offering Healthcare Heroes Free Standard Oil Changes and Enhanced Cleaning (Most Makes and Models)!

April 16, 2020 · Alicia#Free

Mazda wants to thank the healthcare heroes battling Covid 19 with a free standard oil change and enhanced cleaning. Head over here for the press release and here for all of the details and to see which workers eligible.

The program is running through May 4th for most standard make passenger cars, SUVs, and crossovers (exotic vehicles, classic car vehicles, offroad vehicles, vehicles with more than 8 quart engine oil capacity, and other vehicles with special manufacturer requirements are not eligible).
