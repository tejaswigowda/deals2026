Happy Cinco de Mayo! If you're excited to get out and celebrate this fun holiday, of course I found deals!

Z Tejas is offering tacos for just $.55 from 3-7 PM!

All day long they will offer their 6th street margarita or chambord margarita for $5 and Coronas for $2.

Taco Guild is offering up their tequila pork tacos for $1.55 all day.

Sumo Maya will have social hour pricing going on all day in the bar. Their Casamigos tequila margaritas will be $5 and the first 100 people to bring pesos can pay for their margaritas in Mexican currency! 5 pesos equals around $.32, quite a deal for a margarita.

If you just want to celebrate Cindo de Mayo at home with some fresh, homemade guacamole, head to Bashas where you can pick up small hass avocados for just a quarter each!
