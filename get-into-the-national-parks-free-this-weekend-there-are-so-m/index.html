Get Into the National Parks FREE This Weekend! There Are So Many to See in AZ!

April 17, 2015 · Alicia Markham

If you don't have a lot going on this weekend, it could be a perfect time to check out the beautiful National Parks our state has to offer. It always makes me sad when I hear about people who have lived here for awhile and have NEVER been to the Grand Canyon! April 18th and 19th, admission to any National Park is FREE in honor of National Park Week. Head over here for more information.
