Attention Veterans: Make Reservations NOW For Free Veteran's Day Meal at Sassi!

November 4, 2015 · Alicia Markham

Veterans Day is coming up fast, next Wednesday, November 11th. If you know someone who has served in our military (or who currently serves), please let them know about the amazing 3-course dinner Sassi in Scottsdale puts on each year. Call (480) 502-9095 to make reservations, the meal is completely free for all current and former military (and $39 for all other).

Head over here for more information on their facebook page and to see the delicious menu.

Reservations fill up fast! I ran into executive chef Chris Nicosia today at Your Life A to Z (the show will air Thursday at 10) and he reminded me about this fantastic evening. I love that they have a big table for veterans dining alone to enjoy an evening together.

Enjoy!
