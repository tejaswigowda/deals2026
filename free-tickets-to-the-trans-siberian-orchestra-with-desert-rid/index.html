FREE Tickets to the Trans Siberian Orchestra With Desert Ridge or Tempe Marketplace Purchases!

November 4, 2014 · Alicia Markham

If you'd love to see the Trans-Siberian Orchestra this holiday season, head over here to find out how to get free tickets from Desert Ridge Marketplace or Tempe Marketplace. Spend $300+ in one day at any of the stores (they have a grocery store, gas station, Target, tons of restaurants and other stores, and gift cards count) and get two free tickets to the November 30th performance at 3 or 7:30.

You redeem your vouchers for tickets at the Gila River Arena box office (you can go as soon as you get your vouchers to score the best seats).

We went last year and had fun but I have to say I was surprised at how LOUD it was. It is a rock concert!

Enjoy!
