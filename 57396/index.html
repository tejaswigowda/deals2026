Veteran's Day is this Saturday and many valley businesses are celebrating the brave men and women who have fought for our country with freebies!

Check out my top 5 (and even more great deals)! If you have one I haven't mentioned please send it to me (alicia@dealsinaz.com).

1. Cobblestone Auto Spa: Veterans and active duty military will receive a FREE basic oil change and car wash on November 11th! This is a $37.99 value.

2. Sport Clips: Veterans and Active Duty miliary will enjoy a free haircut on 11/11 with proof of service. Even if you’ve never served in the military, keep in mind that $1 of your haircut on 11/11 will go to their help a hero campaign. Head over here for more information.

3. Sassi Scottsdale: For the last time Sassi will be offering a free 3 course meal to all Veterans on 11/11. Unfortunately they’re closing this summer but this is a wonderful opportunity for a great meal at an even better price. Reservations are required, head over here for more information.

4. BJ’s:Veterans and active duty military get a free entree under $12.95 with proof of service today (November 10th) and tomorrow!

5. Ultra-Star Multi-Tainment Center at Ak-Chin: FREE Movie November 10th, 11th, and 12th for current or former military with ID.
