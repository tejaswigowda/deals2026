March 20th is Free Cone Day at Dairy Queen AND Free Rita's Italian Ice Day! Mark Your Calendar!

March 14, 2025 · Alicia#Free#Restaurants

If you're a fan of Dairy Queen, you won't want to miss Free Cone Day! On March 20th everyone can stop in for a FREE vanilla cone (no purchase necessary) at participating locations. I went to the location near 44th street and Thomas in Phoenix and they even let me get a chocolate cone! YUM!

Head over here for more information.

You can also stop by Rita's Italian Ice on March 20th for a FREE 6 oz. cup of your choice. They're featuring their brand new Skittles flavor. Head over here for more information.

In AZ, Rita's locations are in Phoenix (Bell and Tatum) and Glendale.

Enjoy!
