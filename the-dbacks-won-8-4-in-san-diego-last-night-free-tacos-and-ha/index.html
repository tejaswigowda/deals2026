The Dbacks WON 8-4 in San Diego Last Night! Free Tacos and Half Off Pizza!

April 14, 2015 · Alicia Markham

The Dbacks won their first road trip game last night 8-4 vs. the San Diego Padres! That means deals for us!

Everytime the Dbacks score 5 or more runs (win or lose) at home OR AWAY, the next day from 4-6 pm fans can get three free tacos with large drink purchase at Taco Bell. That means today (4/14) is free taco day!

When the Dbacks win (home or away), the next day you can get half off your online order at Papajohns.com with promo code DBACKS50! That means today (4/14) is half off pizza day! I miss the Papa Johns that used to deliver to iyr house!

Personal story: You may want to make sure you tell your significant other about these deals. I was at a dance event with my daughter this weekend and found out that he and our son went out for Jack in the Box Jumbo Jacks...and PAID for them...when it was a free day with large drink purchase since the Dbacks got a home run. Ugh!

Unfortunately there were no home runs last night, so no free Jumbo Jacks today.

Enjoy!
