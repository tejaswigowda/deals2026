Do You Know Your Personality Type? Interesting, FREE Test that Could Help You Understand Your Family Better!

October 29, 2019 · Alicia

Parenting is definitely the hardest job out there, especially parenting tweens and teens. This week I took a free 12 minute test (you can check it out here) that was really helpful. My personality type is ESFJ (there are 16 unique types). I haven't taken the test since college and started thinking it would be interesting to find out how the kids and my husband would score. They took the test (it probably works best with older kids) and the results were fascinating. We all have different types and I truly feel it's great for us to understand where we're all coming from, ESPECIALLY as we try to navigate the tween and teen years.

This is not a diagnosis by any means and I'm not affiliated with their site, I just really appreciated the information!

I hope it helps you too! I would love to know your type!
