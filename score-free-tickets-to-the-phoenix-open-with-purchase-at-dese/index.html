Score Free Tickets to The Phoenix Open With Purchase at Desert Ridge!

January 10, 2022 · Alicia#Deals in Arizona#Free#Happiness#HOT DEAL

If you're thinking about going to the Waste Management Phoenix Open this year, this is a wonderful way to save on admission. Head over here for details about the deal Desert Ridge Marketplace is offering this year. Basically, if you spend $300 in one day at any of their stores (you can combine receipts from more than one store), you'll get 2 free tickets to The Greatest Show on Grass (worth up to $150 if you go Friday or Saturday). Don't forget that you can spend $300 on gift cards (if you shop at Albertsons or Target a lot you know those will get used)!

Tickets to the Open on Monday and Tuesday (February 7th-8th) are free to all.

"Disclaimer:

Same day receipts totaling $300 or more can be redeemed at the Desert Ridge Marketplace 24/7 Security Office. Offer valid while supplies last. Applies to purchases made in the same day, now – January 31, 2021, at Desert Ridge Marketplace retailers and restaurants only. Limit 2 Waste Management Phoenix Open tickets per person for the duration of the promotion. Cannot redeem tickets for additional people. Must be present to redeem. Must be 18 years or older and present a valid ID. Billed monthly services or subscriptions (such as phone bills) do not qualify. Other restrictions may apply.

Security Office Location:

The 24/7 Security Office is located inside the District between Sharetea and Victoria’s Secret PINK. The office is the first door on the right after the public restrooms. Please call 480-419-8101 if you have any troubles finding."

Enjoy!
