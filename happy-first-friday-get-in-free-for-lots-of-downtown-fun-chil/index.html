Happy First Friday! Get In Free For Lots of Downtown Fun (Children's Museum is NOT Participating This Month)!

May 1, 2015 · Alicia Markham

Hooray! Today (10/3) is First Friday! If you don’t have plans, you may want to head to Downtown Phoenix to enjoy the gorgeous weather, some beautiful art, an Ollie the Trolley ride, and some FREE museums! Anyone that says Phoenix has no culture has obviously never been to First Friday!

Visit the Heard Museum for their First Friday Native + You program.Everyone will get into the museum FOR FREE from 6-10 (tickets are usually $18 for adults, $7.50 for kids), and they will be hosting a "Locavore bash" for May Day!

Still have energy? Go to the Phoenix Art Museum for their First Friday event. Everyone will get in for free from 6-10 PM (tickets are usually $15 for adults, $6 for kids).

If it’s date night and you’re leaving the kids at home, head to Adults Night Out at the Arizona Science Center. Adults will get in free from 5:30-9 (tickets are usually $16.95) AND they’ll have an open bar and lots of fun!

I called the Children's Museum and they said they will be participating again in June!

Ennjoy!
