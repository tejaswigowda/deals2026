"To our Valued Guests,

We know this is a stressful and trying time for all of us. We purposefully didn’t send out an email prior out of respect for your inbox. We have a longstanding excellent record with our health department and our commitment to cleanliness and food safety has never been stronger. We hope that you will allow us the honor of feeding and serving you and your families. For over 20 years we have prided ourselves on our kitchen being yours and thank you for your continued support. We have the best guests and we know that together we will get through this time.

Nikki West, CEO and the entire NYPD Pizza family. #Foodislove"

Thank you so much Nikki!
