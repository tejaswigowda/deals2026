It's First Friday! FREE Admission to the Children's Museum of Phoenix, The Heard Museum, The Phoenix Art Museum, and More!

October 3, 2014 · Alicia Markham

Hooray! Today (10/3) is First Friday! If you don't have plans, you may want to head to Downtown Phoenix to enjoy the gorgeous weather, some beautiful art, an Ollie the Trolley ride, and some FREE museums! Anyone that says Phoenix has no culture has obviously never been to First Friday!

Head to the Children's Museum of Phoenix where everyone will get in free from 5-9 PM. Tickets are usually $11 each, so this is quite an opportunity to play for less!

Visit the Heard Museum for their First Friday Native + You program. Everyone will get into the museum FOR FREE from 6-10 (tickets are usually $18 for adults, $7.50 for kids), and there will be music, breakdancing, lowriders, and more!

Still have energy? Go to the Phoenix Art Museum for their First Friday event. Everyone will get in for free from 6-10 PM (tickets are usually $15 for adults, $6 for kids).

If it's date night and you're leaving the kids at home, head to Adults Night Out at the Arizona Science Center. Adults will get in free from 5:30-9 (tickets are usually $16.95) AND they'll have an open bar and lots of fun!

Just to recap, if you take your family of four (two adults, two kids) to the Children's Museum of Phoenix, the Heard Museum, and the Phoenix Art Museum, you'll save a whopping $137 on admission (more if you have more kids).

If it's date night, I'm assuming you won't head to the Children's Museum, but you and your date will save $99.90 on tickets to the Arizona Science Center, the Heard, and the Phoenix Art Museum. AND you're sure to have a great time! The weather couldn't be any more beautiful!

Enjoy!
