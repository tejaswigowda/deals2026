Have You Checked Out Groupon Coupons? A New Way to Save!

December 3, 2015 · Alicia Markham

If you've been reading Alicia's Deals for awhile, you know how much I love Groupon. They offer great deals on fun experiences all over town. There's a new addition to the Groupon site you might not know about called Groupon Coupons. Head over there before you shop to see what kinds of FREE offers you can pick up.

For example, let's say that you're booking a hotel room with Orbitz. Groupon Coupons has codes available that will save you up to an extra $150 off hotel bookings and an extra 15% off hotels.

Want to go to Sam's Club but don't have a membership? Groupon Coupons has a free one-day shopping pass for you.

Thinking of sending Birchbox gifts this year? Groupon Coupons has codes that will get you $10 off your $60 purchase or 20% off your purchase of $50 or more.

Going to Harry & David? Take along a 25% off in store coupon from Groupon Coupons.

It's definitely worth checking out before you shop! You could save at Target, Nordstrom, Sephora, Macy's, Victoria's Secret, Loft, Gap, Payless, and lots of other great stores.
