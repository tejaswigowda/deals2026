TWO FREE Redbox Rental Text Offers! Get Maleficent, Hercules, or Another Fun Rental!

November 4, 2014 · Alicia Markham

Here are Two fun Redbox text offer! Text the word MOVIENIGHT to 727272. You'll get a code good for a completely free one-night rental (you'll have a week to use it).

Then, text the word DEAL to 727272. You should get a code good for a free rental when you buy one.

I just tested the code the DEAL offer sent me online and my code gave me a free rental without buying another one!

They say it's good through 11/26 and I'm not sure if the deal will work the same way without reserving online (in my opinion, the best way to make sure you'll see what you're hoping to see). We all have TWO free movie nights to look forward to (and we don't have to use them today)!

Hope both deals work for you too!
