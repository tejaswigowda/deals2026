Join the FREE Maricopa County Summer Reading Program 'Color Our World'! Great Prizes for All Ages!

June 10, 2025 · Alicia#Deals in Arizona#Free#Happiness

If you like to read, head over here to join the Maricopa County summer reading program called "Color Our World". It's happening at all Maricopa County libraries and is open to readers of all ages (0-100+). You earn points by reading (one minute read equals one point).

Check out the prizes you can earn:

"500 points (while supplies last):

Peter Piper Pizza: personal pan pizza for kids 0-11 (must be redeemed at your library)

Raising Cane’s: free drink for all ages (must be redeemed at your library)

750 points:

AZ State Parks Pass for kids 5-11, teens and adults (must be redeemed at your library)

Ono BBQ free Ono Keiki meal for kids and teens 0-17 (must be redeemed at your library)

Phoenix Mercury, 2 tickets for all ages (redeemed through a link sent through in-game mail)

1,000 points: Free book for all ages (redeemed through the online bookstore, link sent through in-game mail)"

If you read to your child, the time counts for both of you!

I'm honestly most excited for the free AZ State Parks Pass!

Enjoy!
