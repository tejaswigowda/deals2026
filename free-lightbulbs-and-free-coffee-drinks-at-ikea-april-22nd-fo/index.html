Free Lightbulbs and Free Coffee Drinks at Ikea April 22nd for Ikea Family Members (Free to Join)!

April 5, 2017 · Alicia Markham

If you love IKEA, you might want to mark April 22nd on your calendar. They're having the IKEA Sustainable Event and the first 500 Ikea Family members in the doors will get a free 2 pack of LED lightbulbs. Head over here for more information. If you're not an IKEA family member yet you can head over here to join for free.

There's lots of other fun going on too like free coffee (including their new specialty drinks), $20 off your $150 furniture purchase when you bring in your old furniture, and more.

Should be a fun day!
