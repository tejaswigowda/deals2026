Circle K wants to show you love today (February 12th, 2026). All Inner Circle rewards members will get $.40 off each gallon of gas. Head over here for more information and to sign up. Check out the press release they sent over:

Circle K, the global convenience store chain, is helping

Arizona and Nevada residents feel the love this Valentine’s season by saving on fuel

during its upcoming Inner Circle Fuel Celebration. For 24 hours, from midnight to

11:59 p.m. on Thursday, February 12, members of the brand’s free rewards

membership program, Inner Circle, will save 40 cents* off per gallon at more than 600

participating locations across the states.

“This Valentine’s Day, we’re showing our love to Inner Circle members with savings that

go the distance,” said Eddie Quintana, Vice President of Regional Operations, Circle K.

“Whether they’re heading out for a romantic night, a weekend road trip, or just everyday

commutes, our exclusive Inner Circle fuel offer helps members save at the pump and

enjoy a little extra along the way.”

It is easy to enroll in Inner Circle and enjoy the Fuel Celebration savings on February 12.

Simply choose one of the following options:

Text JOIN to 25050**

Download the Circle K app and create an account

Join online via the Circle K website

Enter your mobile number at the pump or at checkout to receive a text with a

quick enrollment link

The Fuel Day Pop-up will only be available at participating Circle K locations in Arizona

and Nevada on February 12, 2026. Customers can find their nearest participating Inner

Circle location by using Circle K’s Store Locator. Following the Fuel Celebration, new

Inner Circle members can continue to enjoy rewards, including 25 cents off per gallon of

fuel on the first five fill-ups, and up to five cents off per gallon every day following.

Membership allows Circle K customers to constantly unlock new experiences,

personalized offers, and more."
