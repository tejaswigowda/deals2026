Just in Time for Spring Break...Pogo Passes Good at a NEW VENUE!

March 7, 2015 · Alicia Markham

Spring Break is next week for many Arizona kids and just in time Pogo Pass secured a new fantastic venue! You can still head over here to get a pass for just $39.98 with code ALICIA (usually around $100).

To make the deal even more exciting, if you buy or renew a pass in March (remember to use code ALICIA) you'll be automatically entered to win 4 tickets to Wicked at Gammage!

Starting March 6th, your pass will get you into Makutu's Island in Chandler twice with your year-long pass (Monday-Friday). Tickets are usually $10 each and here's a description of the place:

"Makutu’s (Ma-coo-two’s) Island, one of America’s largest indoor play places, is a trip into fantasy and adventure for children of all ages and a perfect way to beat the Arizona heat. Designed with a tropical theme, Makutu’s is a lush, exciting indoor “island” with towering trees, climbs, bungee, zip tracker, arcade, café, theater, toddler zone and mysterious caves.

The centerpiece of Makutu’s Island is its magnificent indoor tree. Filled with tunnels, tubes, mole-holes, fire-poles and slides, the magnificent tree offers hours of fun and adventure. Around the tree’s perimeter, guests will find animals carved within its extensive roots and a comical three-animal totem pole. Within the majestic tree, the “banana” slide is a sure thrill as it starts on the forth level in the air and winds more than 35 feet around the outside of the tree to its base.

After exploring the tree, many of Makutu’s guests find themselves in one of the three adjoining play structures including the Blue Fantasy Tree, Pirates Cove and Palm Slide. Less rigorous than the old oak tree, these areas are perfect for the less-experienced island traveler and include tunnels, caves and a zip-line.

In addition to Makutu’s main four play areas, the island also offers its smaller guests a special toddlers area. Oscar’s Lagoon is a slower placed environment of Makutu’s smallest “castaways.” In the Lagoon, toddlers will enjoy three small slides and soft foam blocks."

The POGO Pass will help your family have the ultimate spring break "staycation". Check out what else your pass includes:

Castles and Coasters: Each Pogo Pass holder recieves one (1) visit to Castles~n~Coasters for a *Silver Pass (worth $29.99). This includes unlimited rides and mini golf.

Each Pogo Pass holder receives general admission to the Phoenix Zoo two (2) times. To gain admission to the Zoo, simply present your Pogo Pass at the admission window. Some experiences require an additional fee. Admission to ZooLights and select special events is not included. An adult general admission is usually $20, so this is a $40 value for adults. There is a new baby orangutan that I can’t wait to visit! Head over here to see more adorable pics (or better yet grab your Pogo Pass and visit in person).

Sunsplash: Each pass holder can attend Golfland Sunsplash one (1) time. Your pass is good for one (1) SunSplash General admission ticket. Simply present your Pogo pass at Sunsplash Guest Service window to be scanned to obtain waterpark wristband. Sunsplash is closed for the season but since your pass is good for a FULL YEAR, you can use this next summer. A general admission ticket for adults is usually $29.99.

IDEA museum (used to be Arizona Museum for Youth): Each pass holder can attend i.d.e.a Museum Two (2) times during normal business hours (closed on Monday). Children 1 yrs of age and up are $8 admission if they do not have a valid Pogo Pass. Children are required to wear socks in play area. ** Excludes opening weekend (Fri, Sat, Sun) of opening exhibitions, 3 weekends a year. Simply present your Pogo Pass at admissions desk for entrance. Since admission is usually $8, this adds $16 of value to your POGO pass.

Enchanted Island: Your Pogo Pass is good for a Day Pass four (4) times at Enchanted Island Park Friday and Sundays during regular business hours. Not valid during Enchanted Island special events (I would call ahead before you go). Limited capacity, first come first serve. Some rides may require additional charge. A day pass is usually $17 for anyone under 54 inches, $10 for anyone over.

Sun Devil Athletics: Pogo Pass is a accepted for general admission tickets to ALL of the following ASU athletic home games: Women’s Basketball, Soccer, Wrestling, Volleyball, Gymnastics, Track and Field. **No need to pre-register for tickets to attend. Simply present your Pogo pass at will call or the front gate to gain admissions.

Arizona Diamondbacks: One (1) ticket per game per pass holder for two (2) home games at Chase Field for the MLB Regular Season. Game options for this season will come out soon.

National Comedy Theater: Each pass holder can attend 2 shows. Please call for a reservation. Seating is limited. First come first serve. Note: Please honor your reservations. Reservations will be cancelled for those who are not in their seats 5 minutes prior to show. Tickets are usually $14 each.

Skateland: Each pass holder can attend Skateland two (2) times per month. Skateland has three (3) different locations available to pass holders. Simply present your Pogo Pass card at the front counter to gain admission to Skateland. Skate Rental not included. There are 3 locations (one in Mesa, one in Glendale, and one in Chandler).

Phoenix Rock Gym: Pogo Pass holders can attend Phoenix Rock Gym two (2) times. Simply present your Pogo Pass at the front counter to gain admission to Phoenix Rock Gym. Waiver required for 18 and younger. Limited capacity, first come first serve. *Climbing gear available upon request for $5.

Jump Street: Each Pass holder can attend Phoenix Jump Street two (2) times. Pass is good for a one (1) hour jump session each attendance. NOTE: This one is only available during normal business hours Mondays and Wednesdays.

Stratum Laser Tag: Pass holders can attend stratum two (2) times. Each pass holder receives one (1) complimentary game of laser tag each time they attend. Pass is valid for 12 months. NOTE: Pogo Passes may be used on Mondays and Wednesdays 4:30-close. Pogo Passes may not be used during school holiday breaks or Stratum’s designated holiday hours. No exceptions.

Tempe Paintball: Pogo Pass holders can attend Tempe Paintball one (1) time per month for 12 months that include gun rental, air, and 100 paintballs. Check for seasonal hours and scheduled groups or events before scheduling your visit. Keep in mind that all participants must be 10 years or older, and must sign a waiver before prior to entering the arena. Note: This is not good on Saturdays.

Arizona Rattlers: (1) One ticket per game per pass holder for three (3) Arizona Rattlers home games at the US Airways Center for the AFL regular season. There will be select games posted before the next regular season.

Fat Cats is coming to Gilbert soon and will be part of the POGO Pass (including bowling)!

Enjoy your passes and please note that venues are subject to change!
