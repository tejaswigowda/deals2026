FREE Edible Arrangements Donut Friday at Participating Locations!

May 31, 2018 · Alicia

National Donut Day is getting more and more exciting! Stop by your closest participating Edible Arrangements location to get their "donut" free on Friday (6/1)! Head over here for more information on the Arcadia facebook page, I also saw Chandler, Scottsdale, and North Scottsdale are participating. I would definitely call your store before you make a special trip.

"Crafted with crisp, donut-shaped Granny Smith Apple slices hand-dipped in real, gourmet chocolate, this new treat brings a whole new meaning to freshly made donuts. Don’t miss your chance to try one for free! NOTE: Limit one donut per customer. While supplies last."

Enjoy!
