Celebrate the Grand Opening of Gelato Cimmino in Scottsdale With FREE Gelato on Saturday!

October 18, 2018 · Alicia

Who doesn't love gelato?! Let's all celebrate the amazing dessert this Saturday at the grand opening of Gelato Cimmino in Scottsdale. From 2-10 PM they're offering free gelato to everyone!

Check out the details on their facebook page:

The Gelato Cimmino team is so confident you will fall in love after the first bite, that we are offering FREE gelato during the event. We love Scottsdale and we love our gelato, so we can't wait to introduce the two."

Enjoy!
