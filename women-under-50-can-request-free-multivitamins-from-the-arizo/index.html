Women Under 50 Can Request Free Multivitamins from the Arizona Department of Health Services!

January 4, 2021 · Alicia

Ladies, we all know how important it is to take our vitamins, especially during a pandemic. Head over here to request a free 90 day supply from the Arizona Department of Health Services. You'll answer a few quick health questions then you'll be set to sign up for your free multivitamins. They say they're for women ages 18-45 but they allow you to get them if you're birth year is 1970 or earlier. I've been using these for years, there are no hidden costs!
