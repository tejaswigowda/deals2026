Love This! Donate a Toy at Fashion Square Saturday Morning Get a Free Ticket to Disney on Ice!

December 1, 2017 · Alicia Markham

What a great opportunity to do some good! Head to Fashion Square on Saturday, December 2nd where starting at 9 AM the first 1000 people who bring a toy to donate will get a free ticket to Disney on Ice!

"Saturday, December 2, join the Scottsdale Fire Department from 9am-1pm as they kick off their annual toy drive. The first 1,000 to donate an unwrapped toy at the bin located outside near Thirsty Lion will receive one complimentary voucher to Disney on Ice."

Click here for more information and where to donate if you can't make it on Saturday.

Note: Each person to bring a toy gets a free ticket (so I would have to have four people and four toys to get four tickets)!

Enjoy!
