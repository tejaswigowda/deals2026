FREE Halloween Doughnut at Krispy Kreme if You Wear Your Costume Tuesday!

October 31, 2017 · Alicia Markham

Here's another fun Halloween freebie to keep in mind! Stop by your closest participating Krispy Kreme in costume Tuesday for a FREE doughnut of your choice. Even if you're too old for trick or treating, you can still enjoy a sweet treat in exchange for going to the effort to dress up!

Enjoy!
