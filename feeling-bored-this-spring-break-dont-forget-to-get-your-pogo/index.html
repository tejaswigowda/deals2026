Feeling Bored This Spring Break? Don't Forget to Get Your Pogo Passes!

March 8, 2016 · Alicia Markham

Spring Break is THIS WEEK for many AZ kiddos! If you’re staying in town and looking for fun stuff to do, get a Pogo Pass. Head over here and use code ALICIA and get 60% off (you’ll pay $39.98 instead of $99.99). This is truly one of the best deals in family entertainment out there. You’ll enjoy a FULL YEAR of admissions to places all over town (each place has their own details about how many times you get in with the pass).

The Play Factory is now included with the pass in Desert Ridge. My daughter Kaitlyn had her birthday party here one year and had a great time. Here’s more information about the place:

“Each Pogo Pass holder receives Three (3) “All Day Admission Passes” to Play Factory, during ALL Regular Open Play Hours. “

Locally owned and family operated, The Play Factory is where everyone can climb, jump, bounce, slide, and weave their way through hours of fun exercise and entertainment! We crank out fun, with an all-ages indoor playground, a 20-foot rock wall, a large inflatable slide, and a separate safety zone and bounce house for toddlers 4 and under, even parents can get in on the excitement. Arcade games, an X Box Play, as well as fresh cotton candy and over 9 flavors of shaved-ice sweeten the deal! “

The pass includes so much more too! Passes are good during regular business hours (unless otherwise noted) and venues are subject to change.

Castles and Coasters: Each Pogo Pass holder recieves One (1) visit to Castles~n~Coasters for a *Silver Pass. *Silver pass is good for Unlimited rides and 1 round of miniature golf.

The Phoenix Zoo: Each Pogo Pass holder receives general admission to the Phoenix Zoo two (2) times.

Sunsplash: Each pass holder can attend Golfland Sunsplash one (1) time. Your pass is good for one (1) SunSplash General admission ticket starting May 16th, 2015 when full park opens for the season.

Arizona Diamondbacks: *One (1) ticket per game for Two (2) Arizona Diamondbacks home games. Available home games are posted under available hours.

Phoenix Rock Gym: Pogo Pass holders can attend Phoenix Rock Gym two (2) times.

Skateland: Each pass holder can attend Skateland two (2) times per month. Skateland has three (3) different locations available to pass holders.

Makutus Island: Each Pogo Pass holder receives general admission to Makutu’s Island two (2) times. Available during regular business hours Monday-Friday. Excludes these major holidays: Memorial Day, Independance Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day and Veterans Day.

Fat Cats: *Each Pogo Pass member recieves 1 (one) game of bowling per week, and 1 (One) Glow Golf visit per month. Shoe rental not included. Outside food and drink prohibited. Available Monday-Thursday 11:00am-10:00pm, Fridays 11:00am-5pm. (Excludes Friday’s after 5pm, Saturdays, Sundays and ALL Holidays)

Pump it Up: Each Pogo Pass holder receives general admission to Pump It Up two (2) times. (Tempe and Peoria locations). Available Monday – Friday, excludes all major holidays and special events.

IDEA Museum: Each pass holder can attend i.d.e.a Museum two (2) times during normal business hours.

Arizona Rattlers: *One (1) ticket per game for Three (3) Arizona Rattlers home games. Available home games are posted under available hours.

ASU Athletics: Pogo Pass is a accepted for general admission tickets to ALL of the following ASU athletic home games: Women’s Basketball, Soccer, Wrestling, Volleyball, Gymnastics, Track and Field

Phoenix Mercury: *One (1) ticket per game, for Three (3) Phoenix Mercury home games. Available home games are posted under available hours.

Tempe Paintball: *Each Pogo Pass member can attend Tempe Paintball one (1) time per month. Each paintball visit includes gun rental, air, and 100 paintballs. Each Airsoft visit includes admission, equipment rental package and 200 BB’s. Available regular business hours excluding Saturdays. Tempe Paintball strongly suggest that you make an online reservation to ensure availability of equipment. Please click on the link below to book your visit http://bit.ly/1TGGMLW.

Enchanted Island: Your Pogo Pass is good for a Day Pass four (4) times at Enchanted Island Park. Limited capacity, first come first serve. Some rides may require additional charge. Good Fridays and Sundays, excluding special events.

Stratum Laser Tag: Pogo Pass holders can attend Stratum Laser Tag two (2) separate times, Pogo Passes may be used on Mondays and Wednesdays 4:30-close. Pogo Passes may not be used during school holiday breaks or Stratum’s designated holiday hours.

You could do so much this spring break!

Enjoy!
