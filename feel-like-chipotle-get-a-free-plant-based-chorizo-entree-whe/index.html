Feel Like Chipotle? Get a FREE Plant-Based Chorizo Entree When You Buy One With Code!

January 5, 2022 · Alicia#EXPIRED

If you've been wanting to try Chipotle's new plant-based Chorizo, you'll love this! Head over here or use the app and use code JUJUPBC to get a free plant-based Chorizo entree when you buy one (you can do half Chorizo/half another protein that's the same price or lower if you'd like)! Note: Both entrees must be Plant-Based Chorizo.

This was working for me today, please let me know if they run out of redemptions!
