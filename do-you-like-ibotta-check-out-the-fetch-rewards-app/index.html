Do You Like Ibotta? Check Out the Fetch Rewards App!

February 7, 2020 · Alicia

If you love Apps that pay you back like Ibotta, head over here to download Fetch Rewards. Use code APREWARDS to get a 3000 point bonus.

I just loaded a picture of a Costco receipt I had in my purse and already have 3750 points. At 5000 points you can redeem rewards like a $5 Ulta or AMC gift card (there are higher point rewards too)!

"Fetch Rewards is a free grocery savings app that rewards you just for snapping pictures of your receipts. That’s really it. Free rewards on groceries on thousands of products every day, no matter where you get your groceries. Just scan your receipts and let us do all the work."

This is WAY easier in Ibotta in that there's way less work looking at deals. You literally just load a picture of the receipt...that's it.
