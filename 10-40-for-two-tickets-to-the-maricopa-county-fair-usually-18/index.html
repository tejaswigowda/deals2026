$10.40 for Two Tickets to the Maricopa County Fair (Usually $18)!

April 12, 2017 · Alicia Markham

The Maricopa County Fair opens today (4/12) and goes through Sunday. If you'd like to stop by and enjoy the food and fun, head over here to get two tickets for $10.40 with code SALE3 (usually $18). You can also get four tickets for $20 with code SALE3, usually $36.

"Where else but the Maricopa County Fair can someone eat one of Steve's Flaming Hot Turkey Legs, ride a ferris wheel, and bid on livestock all in a single afternoon? This annual celebration of fun and agriculture takes over the Arizona State Fairgrounds with more than 35 carnival rides and non-stop entertainment. Fair festivities set the carnivalesque tone with everything from musical performances to magic shows to a High Dive Show. Attendees also get to meet costumed characters and watch competitions, including Monster Truck races and motorcycle races."

Keep in mind that kids under 7 are free.

Enjoy!
