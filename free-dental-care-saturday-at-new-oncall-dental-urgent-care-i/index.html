FREE DENTAL CARE Saturday at New OnCall Dental Urgent Care in Tempe!

October 16, 2014 · Alicia Markham

If you know someone who is in need of a dentist and might not have dental insurance, I hope you'll share this deal with them! Head over here for more information about the GRAND OPENING of the new OnCall Dental urgent care Saturday, October 18th from 10AM-2PM. They'll offer one free dental cleaning, emergency exam, or surface filling to anyone who comes to the grand opening celebration. They'll also offer $10 flu shots and have the SnoShack on hand for everyone there.

I was worried about huge wait times. They told me that they weren't expecting long waits but if there happened to be huge wait times, they would honor the free service and schedule individuals on a different day!

Here's a little bit more information about the place:

About OnCall Dental Urgent Care

"OnCall Dental Urgent Care treat all patients, not just emergencies. People working regular 9-5 day shifts with little schedule flexibility will now have somewhere to go to receive quality regular and emergency dental care during evenings and on weekends. Our goal is to help the medical community by treating their patients with dental emergencies. Emergency rooms, medical urgent care facilities and family practice offices are being flooded with dental emergencies. All they can do is write a prescription for pain medication, antibiotics and refer the patient to their dentist. These dental emergency visits are costing hospitals millions of dollars every year and all they can give is a temporary solution. Now those same patients can be referred to OnCall where they can be seen the same day for extractions, root canals or whatever treatment is needed."
