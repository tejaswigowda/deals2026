We all spend a ton of our hard earned cash at the grocery stores and it's truly one of my favorite places to save money. Head over here to download the ibotta app on your iphone or tablet.

They'll give you money back for buying things most likely already on your grocery list like cereal, peanut butter, paper towels, beer and wine, and even produce. They offer cash back if you shop at Fry's, Target, Walmart, Safeway, and Albertsons. It makes shopping a little bit more fun and the savings are on top of sales and coupons. I've had readers save literally hundreds (two readers I've heard from have saved $2000) with this app.

Speaking of apps, make sure you have the app to the store you shop at most. Safeway and Albertsons offer Just 4 U personalized deals based on your shopping habits while Fry's and Target offer deals you can only score if you use their app.

Fry's also offers a freebie most Fridays and a $2 or $3 off facebook coupon most Wednesdays you can load directly to your VIP card.

You'll be amazed how many restaurants have apps these days. The savings add up so quickly, especially if you visit one restaurant all the time.
