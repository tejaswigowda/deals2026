Have the Starbucks App? Order a Grande or Larger-Sized Drink With It Today (9/22) and Get an Offer for a FREE Drink 9/25-9/26 (Up to $10 Value)!

September 22, 2021 · Alicia#EXPIRED

Here's a fun buy one get one! If you have the Starbucks app, go to your offers and click "details" then "order now" to buy a grande or larger sized drink TODAY ONLY (9/22). You'll automatically get a free drink 9/25-9/26 (up to a $10 value though I am wondering what Starbucks drink is $10).

Check out the details:

"Pumpkin season is here. Spice up your week with a delicious free sip. Grab any grande (or larger) handcrafted beverage in the app on 9/22 and get one free drink, from 9/25–9/26.

A coupon will be automatically loaded to your account on 9/25.

COUPON INSTRUCTIONS:

To access your coupon, go to the “Offers" tab and click details.

To redeem, ask your barista to apply your coupon at the register.

On 9/22/2021, buy a grande or larger handcrafted drink at a participating store via mobile order and get a free drink. Free drink fulfilled as a coupon automatically loaded to your Starbucks Rewards account on 9/25/2021. Coupon valid from 9/25/2021–9/26/2021 at participating stores. Max coupon value $10 (you are responsible for any customizations exceeding this amount). Not valid on purchases of hot brewed coffee or tea, Starbucks Reserve beverages, alcohol, or ready-to-drink beverages. Cannot be combined with other offers or discounts. Offer valid for one-time use. Coupon cannot be redeemed for alcoholic or ready-to-drink beverages."

Enjoy!
