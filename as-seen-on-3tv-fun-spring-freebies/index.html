Spring is officially here and there are all kinds of fun freebies we can enjoy to celebrate so many things!

I love my doughnuts and Krispy Kreme wants everyone to enjoy Mondays and be sweet to themselves this Spring! Everyone can stop in any Monday through May 24th to get a FREE medium coffee and original glazed doughnut, no purchase required.

Krispy Kreme wants us all to be sweet to our communities and they're offering a FREE original glazed doughnut every day through the end of the year to anyone with a Covid vaccination card. We didn't really need an extra incentive to get vaccinated…hey it's sweet!

If you'd love a colorful experience, the Crayola Experience Store is offering free reservations for a 32 pack of crayons in honor of National Crayon Day. The best part is you get to pick all 32 colors yourself from the huge wall of crayons! There are reservations available through May 26th and they're going fast, head over here to get yours.

If you want to brighten someone's day, Hallmark wants to help! All Crown Gold Members will receive a FREE Just Because card (up to a $2.99 value) each month through June! There is nothing better than a great hand-written card in the mailbox! There is absolutely no charge to become a Hallmark Crown Gold member, head over here for more information!

Spring always makes me think of babies and if you're expecting, think about heading over here to make a registry at Target. Just for adding items to your list, they'll give you a FREE welcome kit worth $120 full of samples and coupons. They have the kits available at guest services, and they're definitely worth picking up!

Enjoy!
