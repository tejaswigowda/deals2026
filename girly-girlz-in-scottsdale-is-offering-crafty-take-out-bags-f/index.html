Girly Girlz in Scottsdale is Offering Crafty Take-Out Bags for Girls and Boys Including Crafts, Dessert, and More for $12!

April 3, 2020 · Alicia

If the kids are looking for something fun to do, head over here to check out the Crafty Take-Out sets available from Girly Girlz in Scottsdale. For $12 your child will get an age-appropriate themed craft, dessert to decorate, with frosting and toppings, and a coloring page with crayons. The themes are Unicorn, Mermaid/Under the Sea, Princess, JoJo, Descendants, and Gender Neutral Canvas Painting.

They are also offering 25% off items in their cute online store with code SHOPSMALL.

Once they receive your order, they'll contact you to set up a curbside pick-up time.
