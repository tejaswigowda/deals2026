Free Chips and Guacamole from Chipotle!

July 5, 2016 · Alicia Markham

Head over here to join Chiptopia if you go to Chipotle a lot! You'll get a free order of chips and guacamole when you purchase an entree next time you go in and use the app. You'll also get a free entree after your fifth visit in a month (one visit per day)!

Enjoy!
