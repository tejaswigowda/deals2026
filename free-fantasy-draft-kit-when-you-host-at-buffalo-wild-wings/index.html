Free Fantasy Draft Kit When You Host at Buffalo Wild Wings!

August 23, 2016 · Alicia Markham

Still figuring out where to host your fantasy football draft party? Head over here for more information about the draft kit you'll get for free when you request to have your party at Buffalo Wild Wings.

You'll get a draft board an player stickers, a brag flag, a last place license plate frame, a pocket schedule, a turf drink sleeve, and coupons. This is while supplies last only. You can also get freebies at Hooters (personally I think Buffalo Wild Wings sounds better but hey, I thought I should mention it).
