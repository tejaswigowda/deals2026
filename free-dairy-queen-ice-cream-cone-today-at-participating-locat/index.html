FREE Dairy Queen Ice Cream Cone Today at Participating Locations!

March 20, 2017 · Alicia Markham

Head to your closest participating Dairy Queen today (3/20) for FREE cone day (note mall locations are not participating)! Everyone who stops in will get a free soft serve cone, no purchase required. They will be accepting donations for Children's Miracle Network hospitals and I would definitely call your location to make sure they're participating before you make a special trip.
