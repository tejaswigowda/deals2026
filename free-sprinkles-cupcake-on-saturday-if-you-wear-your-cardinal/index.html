Free Sprinkles Cupcake on Saturday if You Wear Your Cardinals Gear!

September 7, 2016 · Alicia Markham

Here's a freebie worth showing up for! Stop into Sprinkles Cupcakes in Scottsdale for a free red velvet on Saturday, September 10th when you wear your Cardinals jersey, shirt, or hat.

Check out the info they sent me:

"WHO: Sprinkles Cupcakes

WHAT: Free Cardinals red velvet cupcake for fans wearing Cardinals jerseys, shirts and hats. Limit one free cupcake per guest.

WHEN: Saturday, September 10th, 2016

9 a.m. to 9 p.m.

WHERE: Sprinkles Cupcakes

4501 N. Scottsdale Rd.

Scottsdale, AZ 85251

Sprinkles is located on the northeast corner of Scottsdale and Camelback roads

WHY: To kick off the football weekend Sprinkles is showing their spirit by offering guests one free Cardinals red velvet cupcake to fans wearing Cardinals jerseys on September 10th, the day before the big game. The first game for the Arizona Cardinals is happening Sunday, September 11th, against the New England Patriots. There is a limit to one cupcake per customer."

Thanks Hannah!
