HOT! Comp Train Tickets Available for Country Splash at Big Surf!

April 26, 2017 · Alicia Markham

This is an awesome freebie you need an app for. Head over here to download the Goldstar app on iTunes and here to download it on Google Play.

Then on the app look for Country Splash at Big Surf! They have a limited number of comp train tickets available (you will pay an $8.50 service fee). Tickets are usually $35 each!

"What's better than good food, cold beer and great country music? Good food, cold beer and great country music at a water park! The Country Splash Music Festival is taking over Tempe's Big Surf Waterpark for a day of honky-tonk fun in the sun. See country stars like Michael Ray (No. 1s "Think a Little Less," "Kiss You in the Morning") and Brooke Eden ("Daddy's Money") on the stage, join in some line dancing, test your skills on the mechanical bull or just cool off on the waterslides -- all while enjoying some top-notch barbecue and beer."

So fun!

Enjoy!
