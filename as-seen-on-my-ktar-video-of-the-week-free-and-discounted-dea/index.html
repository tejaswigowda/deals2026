As Seen on My KTAR Video of the Week...FREE and Discounted Deals on Downtown Fun This Week!

June 3, 2014 · Alicia Markham

You might already know that I do a weekly video for KTAR's website. This week I talked about all of the fun going on downtown this weekend (a lot of the fun is FREE)!

Phoenix Comicon is coming June 5th-8th. You can head over here to get discounted tickets through Wednesday night. Why pay full price when you don't have to?!

If you’ve never been to First Friday in Downtown Phoenix, this might be a great week to try it out!

Admission is free for everyone to the Children’s Museum of Phoenix from 5-9 PM and the Phoenix Art Museum from 6-10 PM. Adults 18 and over get in free to the Arizona Science Center for their Adults Night Out.

There’s a free trolley too!

Also on Friday keep in mind that it's National Doughnut Day! On your way downtown stop at Krispy Kreme for a free doughnut (no purchase required) or Dunkin' Donuts (buy a drink to get a free donut).

There's lots of fun at the Dbacks game too. The first 20,000 fans through the gates will get a free Martin Prado bobblehead on June 7th.

If you feel like eating out while you're downtown, head over here to check out Local Dines. They have half price certificates to all kinds of restaurants you'll enjoy like Nobuo at Teeter House, Province, the Vig, and more!

Enjoy!
