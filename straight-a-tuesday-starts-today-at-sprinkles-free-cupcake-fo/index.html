Straight A Tuesday Starts Today at Sprinkles! Free Cupcake for Students With A on Paper or Test! BOGO Free Cupcake Mondays for Teachers Too!

September 1, 2015 · Alicia Markham

I love that they're bringing this back starting today! Head to Sprinkles Scottsdale with your students Tuesdays in September. If they bring an A on a test or paper, they'll get a FREE cupcake!

Sprinkles love teachers too. On Mondays when they flash a school ID, they'll enjoy a free cupcake when they buy one.

Sprinkles Scottsdale is located at 4501 N. Scottsdale Road on the northeast corner of Scottsdale and Camelback roads, across from Scottsdale Fashion Square.

Enjoy!
