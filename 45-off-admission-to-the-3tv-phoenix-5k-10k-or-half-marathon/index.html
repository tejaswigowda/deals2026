3TV Phoenix 10K and Half Marathon

Racers don't have to carry walk-talkies or get tangled up in tin-can-telephones in order to keep in touch with their loved ones during the 3TV Phoenix 10K and Half Marathon. There, the event's media team captures each racers' triumphant trip across the finish line on video. The race ends with an afteparty, complete with medals for the half marathon finishers, the fastest runners, and the runner who took the secretest coffee breaks.
