YAHOO! Veterans and Active Duty Military Get FREE Sea World Tickets for Themselves and Up to Three Dependents!

May 21, 2019 · Alicia

If you know anyone who has served our country in the military, I hope you'll let them know about this AMAZING deal at Sea World! Active Duty Military can register here to get free tickets for themselves and up to three direct dependents and Veterans can register here. You must reserve your tickets before you go. Check out the information for Vets:

"Free Admission for Veterans and Guests

For your service and sacrifice, we thank and salute you.

We hold the men and women that have served in our armed forces in the highest regard, and are honored to host them at our parks as our guests. Through our Waves of Honor program, former military personnel and up to three guests may enjoy one complimentary admission to one of the following parks: SeaWorld® in San Diego, Orlando or San Antonio; or Busch Gardens Tampa Bay or Williamsburg. Blockout dates apply. Please see our FAQs below for more information.

  • Offer extends to U.S. Military Veterans, retired Veterans and retirees who have honorably served and includes one complimentary admission per promotional period per Veteran and up to three guests.
  • To take advantage of these offers, click the button to verify your Veteran status through ID.me. (Spouses must log-in under the Veteran's account in order to take advantage of the guest tickets).
  • The ticket(s) must be obtained online by June 9, 2019, and redeemed at an eligible park for visitation by July 15, 2019. The Veteran must be present with their 3 guests upon redemption at the park for visitation. Blockout dates apply (including June 1 and June 15, 2019, for SeaWorld San Diego).

For information on the Waves of Honor Active Duty Offer, please click here." AWESOME! Thanks Alex!
