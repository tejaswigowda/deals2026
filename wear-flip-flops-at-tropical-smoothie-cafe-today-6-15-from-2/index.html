Wear Flip Flops at Tropical Smoothie Cafe Today (6/15) from 2-7 for a FREE Smoothie! Donations Accepted for Camp Sunshine!

June 15, 2018 · Alicia

It's National Flip Flop Day and that means free smoothies! Wear your favorite pair of flip flops to a participating Tropical Smoothie Cafe near you (6/15 from 2-7 PM) and get a FREE 16 oz. Sunshine smoothie (Mango, banana, orange, pineapple & orange juice). They will be accepting donations for Camp Sunshine, a place where kids with life threatening illnesses can go for a break with their families. Head over here for more information.

What a win/win!
